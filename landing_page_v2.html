Questions, Answered

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Yes. Forward your current number to your Diazites AI receptionist, or get a dedicated number in your city.

How does the AI know about my business?

Tell us your services, hours, pricing, and FAQs during setup — the AI learns your business in minutes.

Can it transfer calls to a real person?

Yes. Set the rules — hot transfers, after-hours routing, or escalate when the caller needs a human.

Can it book directly into my calendar?

Yes. Connect your calendar and the AI books appointments directly into your schedule with automatic confirmations.

Can it make outbound calls?

Yes. Follow up with leads, send appointment reminders, and run outbound campaigns.

What happens if the AI doesn't know an answer?

It gracefully takes the caller's info and notifies you — or transfers to your team. It never guesses.

You can be live in minutes. Pick your number, choose a voice, and your AI starts answering today.
