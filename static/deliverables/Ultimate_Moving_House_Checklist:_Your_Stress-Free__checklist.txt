## Moving House Ultimate Checklist

### 8 Weeks Before Moving
- [ ] Create a moving binder or digital folder for all documents, receipts, and contacts.
- [ ] Set a moving budget and start tracking expenses.
- [ ] Research and book a moving company or rent a truck.
- [ ] Declutter: sort items into keep, donate, sell, and trash.
- [ ] Start using up perishable food items to reduce waste.
- [ ] Notify landlord (if renting) of your move-out date.
- [ ] Obtain packing supplies: boxes, tape, bubble wrap, markers, labels.
- [ ] Take inventory of all rooms and valuable items.

### 6 Weeks Before Moving
- [ ] Begin packing non-essential items (seasonal decorations, rarely used books).
- [ ] Change of address: notify USPS, banks, insurance, subscriptions.
- [ ] Schedule utility disconnections for current home (electric, gas, internet, water).
- [ ] Schedule utility connections for new home (same as above).
- [ ] Arrange for school transfers and obtain records if needed.
- [ ] Book pet sitter or boarding for moving day.
- [ ] Collect important documents: passports, birth certificates, medical records.
- [ ] Measure furniture and new rooms to ensure fit.

### 4 Weeks Before Moving
- [ ] Confirm moving company details and finalize contract.
- [ ] Pack seasonal clothes and items not needed in next 4 weeks.
- [ ] Label boxes by room and contents clearly.
- [ ] Arrange for parking permits or elevator reservations if applicable.
- [ ] Notify employer and update HR with new address.
- [ ] Cancel local memberships (gym, library, clubs) or transfer them.
- [ ] Plan for large items: disassemble furniture, drain appliances.
- [ ] Create a "first night" box: toiletries, change of clothes, chargers, snacks.

### 2 Weeks Before Moving
- [ ] Pack all non-essential kitchen items and linens.
- [ ] Defrost and clean refrigerator/freezer.
- [ ] Transfer prescription medications to a new pharmacy.
- [ ] Update driver's license and vehicle registration address.
- [ ] Notify insurance companies (home, auto, health) of move.
- [ ] Arrange for cleaning services for old home (if needed).
- [ ] Confirm moving day schedule with movers and helpers.
- [ ] Start using up cleaning supplies and toiletries.

### 1 Week Before Moving
- [ ] Pack a "essentials suitcase" for each family member.
- [ ] Empty and clean all trash cans.
- [ ] Take down curtains, blinds, and wall decorations.
- [ ] Drain garden hoses and prep outdoor items.
- [ ] Service your car for the trip (if moving long distance).
- [ ] Confirm final walk-through with landlord or buyer.
- [ ] Gather all keys, garage openers, and access cards.

### Moving Day
- [ ] Wake up early and do a final walk-through of every room.
- [ ] Check all closets, cabinets, and attic for forgotten items.
- [ ] Disconnect and safely pack electronics (TV, computer, router).
- [ ] Keep moving binder, essentials box, and valuables with you.
- [ ] Supervise movers and direct them to correct rooms at new home.
- [ ] Take photos of any existing damage in new home for records.
- [ ] Confirm utilities are on at new home.
- [ ] Unpack essential items first: beds, toiletries, kitchen basics.

### After Moving (1-2 Weeks)
- [ ] Change locks on new home doors.
- [ ] Set up home security system (if applicable).
- [ ] Register children in new schools.
- [ ] Find new doctors, dentists, and veterinarians.
- [ ] Explore neighborhood: locate grocery stores, pharmacies, hospitals.
- [ ] Unpack room by room, starting with bedrooms and kitchen.
- [ ] Dispose of packing materials properly (recycle boxes).
- [ ] Update your address on all remaining accounts and services.
- [ ] Leave a review for your moving company.
- [ ] Celebrate your successful move!
