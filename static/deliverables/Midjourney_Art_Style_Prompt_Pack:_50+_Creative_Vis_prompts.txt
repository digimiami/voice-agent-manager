### Bonus: Customize Your Style
Add these modifiers to any prompt:
- `--s 250` (stylize: 0-1000, higher = more artistic)
- `--c 10` (chaos: 0-100, higher = more surprising)
- `--iw 2` (image weight: if using an image reference)
- `--no [element]` (exclude something, e.g., --no text)
- `--ar 16:9` (aspect ratio)

**Pro tip:** Combine two styles for unique results, e.g., "cyberpunk + watercolor".
