## Sales Page Alchemist: 30 Expert Copywriting Prompts

### Chapter 1: Headline & Hook Mastery (5 prompts)
1. **The Curiosity Gap Headline** – "Write 3 headlines that tease a specific, surprising result your reader wants. Example: 'How I [achieved X] in [short time] without [common pain point].'"
2. **The Pain-Promise Headline** – "Craft a headline that names the #1 frustration of your audience and promises relief. Structure: 'Stop [pain] and finally [desired outcome].'"
3. **The Social Proof Headline** – "Create a headline using a specific number or statistic from your success case. Example: 'How [Number] of [Audience] Achieved [Result] Using [Product].'"
4. **The Command Headline** – "Write an urgent, action-oriented headline that starts with a powerful verb. Example: 'Transform Your [X] in 30 Days—Here’s How.'"
5. **The Question Headline** – "Pose a question your ideal customer asks themselves daily. Example: 'Are You Making This Costly Mistake in Your [Area]?'"

### Chapter 2: Feature-to-Benefit Transformation (5 prompts)
6. **The 'So What?' Test** – "For each feature, write a benefit that answers 'So what?' Start with 'This means you can...' Example: Feature: '24/7 support' → Benefit: 'This means you never lose sleep over technical glitches.'"
7. **The Emotional Benefit** – "Describe how your product makes the user feel. Use sensory words: 'Imagine the relief of...' or 'Feel the confidence when...'"
8. **The Before/After Benefit** – "Write a short paragraph contrasting life before vs. after using your product. Focus on tangible outcomes."
9. **The Specific Number Benefit** – "Quantify the benefit: 'Save 10 hours/week' or 'Boost revenue by 34%.' Use real data if possible."
10. **The Benefit List** – "Create a bulleted list of 5-7 benefits, each starting with a strong verb (e.g., 'Eliminate...', 'Double...', 'Simplify...')."

### Chapter 3: Objection Smashing (5 prompts)
11. **The Price Objection** – "Write a paragraph that justifies your price by comparing it to the cost of inaction or alternatives. Example: 'Failing to solve [problem] costs you $X/year. Our solution pays for itself in [time].'"
12. **The Trust Objection** – "Address skepticism by listing guarantees, testimonials, or certifications. Start with: 'We understand your hesitation. That’s why we offer...'"
13. **The Time Objection** – "Respond to 'I don’t have time' by showing how your product saves time or requires minimal effort. Example: 'Set up in 5 minutes, then let automation do the work.'"
14. **The 'It Won’t Work for Me' Objection** – "Use a story of a client with similar doubts who got results. Include specific details."
15. **The Comparison Objection** – "Create a side-by-side comparison chart of your product vs. top competitors, highlighting your unique advantages."

### Chapter 4: Storytelling for Sales (5 prompts)
16. **The Origin Story** – "Share the 'why' behind your product—a personal struggle, a moment of insight, or a client’s desperate request. Make it relatable."
17. **The Client Hero Story** – "Tell a story of a client’s journey from pain to success, using their own words (with permission). Include the turning point where your product helped."
18. **The 'Before' Scene** – "Paint a vivid picture of the frustration your audience feels before buying. Use dialogue, sensory details, and specific pain points."
19. **The 'After' Scene** – "Describe the ideal outcome in rich detail—how their life looks, feels, and sounds after using your product."
20. **The Transformation Bridge** – "Write a short narrative that connects the 'before' to the 'after,' emphasizing the ease or speed of the change."

### Chapter 5: Social Proof & Credibility (5 prompts)
21. **The Micro-Testimonial** – "Collect 3 one-sentence testimonials that highlight specific results. Format: '[Name] achieved [result] using [product].'"
22. **The Case Study Mini** – "Write a 3-paragraph case study: problem, solution, result. Include a quote and a key metric."
23. **The Social Media Proof** – "Embed screenshots of positive comments, shares, or user-generated content from social platforms."
24. **The Expert Endorsement** – "If applicable, feature a quote from an industry expert or influencer who trusts your product."
25. **The Numbers Game** – "List impressive stats: 'Over 10,000 happy customers,' '4.8-star average rating,' '99% satisfaction rate.'"

### Chapter 6: Irresistible CTAs & Scarcity (5 prompts)
26. **The Benefit-Driven CTA** – "Create a button text that states the benefit, not just the action. Example: 'Start My Free Trial' → 'Unlock My 14-Day Free Access.'"
27. **The Urgency CTA** – "Add a time-limited element: 'Claim Your 50% Discount – Offer Ends [Date].' Or use countdown timer language."
28. **The Risk Reversal CTA** – "Pair your CTA with a guarantee: 'Add to Cart – 100% Money-Back Guarantee.'"
29. **The Soft CTA** – "For hesitant buyers: 'Learn More' or 'See How It Works' with low commitment."
30. **The Final Push** – "Write a closing paragraph that combines scarcity, social proof, and a clear call to action. Example: 'Join 5,000+ satisfied customers. But hurry—only 50 spots left at this price. Click below to secure your spot.'"

### Bonus: Sales Page Structure Template (for easy assembly)
- **Headline** (use prompt 1-5)
- **Subheadline** (expand on the promise)
- **Problem Agitation** (2-3 sentences, prompt 18)
- **Solution Introduction** (your product as the answer)
- **Benefits List** (prompt 10)
- **Storytelling Section** (prompt 16-20)
- **Objection Handling** (prompt 11-15)
- **Social Proof** (prompt 21-25)
- **Guarantee/Risk Reversal**
- **Final CTA with Scarcity** (prompt 26-30)
