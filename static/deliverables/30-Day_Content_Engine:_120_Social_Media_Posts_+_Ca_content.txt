30-Day Content Engine: 120 Social Media Posts + Canva Templates

## System Overview
30 days of ready-made content: 30 Instagram posts, 30 LinkedIn posts, 30 Twitter threads, 30 TikTok scripts. Each with hooks, copy, hashtags, and optimal posting times. Plus 10 Canva templates.

## Content Pillars (Monthly Rotation)
Pillar 1: Value Education (Days 1-10) - Teach your audience something useful
Pillar 2: Social Proof and Stories (Days 11-20) - Showcase results and build trust
Pillar 3: Engagement and Conversion (Days 21-30) - Interact and drive action

## Week 1: Value Education (Days 1-10)
### Day 1: The Hook
- Instagram (7AM): "This one mindset shift changed everything." 5-slide carousel.
- LinkedIn (8:30AM): "I spent 5 years learning this. You can learn it in 3 minutes."
- Twitter (9AM): 5-tweet thread: "The 5-step framework that doubled my productivity"
- TikTok (12PM): 45-sec: "The #1 mistake and how to fix it"

### Day 2-3: Industry Insight + How-To Tutorial
- Instagram Reel: 15-sec quick tutorial with trending audio
- LinkedIn: "How to [task] in 5 steps" with personal example
- TikTok: 60-sec detailed tutorial with screen recording

### Day 4-5: Common Mistakes + Tools
- Carousel: "5 [topic] mistakes costing you [result]"
- List post: "My 5 non-negotiable tools for [activity]"

### Day 6-7: Framework + Data
- Infographic carousel showing framework
- Stat-based post with bold number and analysis

### Day 8-10: Beginner Guide, Expert Roundup, Checklist
- Step-by-step carousel roadmap
- Quotes from 5 experts
- "[X] things to check" checklist format

## Week 2: Social Proof (Days 11-20)
### Day 11: Milestone
- Celebration graphic with real stats
- Cross-platform announcement

### Day 12-13: Case Study + Behind Scenes
- "How [client] achieved [result]" carousel
- BTS photo/video of workspace

### Day 14-15: Failure + Transformation
- "My biggest failure" honest carousel
- Before/after transformation story

### Day 16-17: Appreciation + Client Spotlight
- Heartfelt community thank you post
- Feature customer story with results

### Day 18-20: Team, UGC, Year Review
- Team introduction
- Customer testimonial repost
- "[Year] in review" annual reflection

## Week 3: Engagement (Days 21-30)
### Day 21-22: Question + Free Resource
- "What's your biggest challenge?" engagement bait
- Free lead magnet with comment to get

### Day 23-24: Live + Limited Offer
- "Going live [date/time]" event post
- "24-hour flash sale" urgency graphic

### Day 25-26: Product Feature + Testimonials
- "What's inside [product]" feature carousel
- "Don't take my word" testimonial carousel

### Day 27-28: Webinar + Comparison
- "Free webinar: [topic]" registration
- "[Option A] vs [Option B]" comparison

### Day 29-30: Future Vision + Final CTA
- Industry predictions for next year
- "Ready to start?" final CTA

## Canva Templates Included (10)
1. Quote Card - Bold quote on gradient background
2. Step-by-Step Carousel - 5-slide tutorial template
3. Before/After - Side-by-side comparison with metrics
4. Stat Highlight - Large number with accent color
5. Product Showcase - Hero image with 3 feature bullets
6. Testimonial Card - Pull quote with star rating
7. Announcement - Bold headline with celebration elements
8. Checklist - Vertical checklist with checkmarks
9. Comparison - Split layout showing two options
10. Polls/Questions - Interactive design with emoji choices

## Optimal Posting Times
- Instagram: Mon-Fri 7-9AM or 6-8PM
- LinkedIn: Tue-Thu 8-10AM, Sat 10AM
- Twitter: Mon-Fri 9AM-12PM
- TikTok: Tue-Thu 7-9AM, Fri-Sat 6-8PM
