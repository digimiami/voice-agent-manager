📋 HOW TO INSTALL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Open the Google Sheets link (sent separately)
2. Go to File → Make a copy
3. Save to your Google Drive
4. Start entering your data!

📊 INCLUDED METRICS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
  • ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)
  • Churn Rate
  • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
  • Lifetime Value (LTV)
  • LTV:CAC Ratio
  • Active Users (DAU/MAU)
  • Revenue Growth Rate
  • Burn Rate
  • Runway

🎯 HOW TO USE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Enter your monthly revenue in the "Revenue" tab
2. Add customer counts in the "Customers" tab
3. All KPIs auto-calculate on the dashboard
4. Use the chart tabs for investor presentations
