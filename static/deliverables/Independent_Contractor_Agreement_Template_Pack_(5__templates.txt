## Product Overview
This pack includes 5 complete contractor agreement templates (Short Form, Long Form, Creative/IP Intensive, Service-Based, and International) plus 15+ supporting clauses and checklists.

### Template 1: Short Form Agreement (Single Project)
- **Scope of Work Clause**: Define deliverables, milestones, and deadlines.
- **Payment Terms**: Fixed fee, payment schedule (e.g., 50% upfront, 50% upon completion), late payment penalty (1.5% monthly).
- **IP Rights**: Work-for-hire assignment; contractor retains pre-existing IP.
- **Confidentiality**: Mutual NDA clause with 2-year term.
- **Termination**: Either party may terminate with 7 days' written notice; payment for work completed.
- **Independent Contractor Status**: No benefits, taxes, or insurance provided.
- **Governing Law**: State of [State].

### Template 2: Long Form Agreement (Ongoing/Retainer)
- **Scope of Work**: Detailed description with change order process.
- **Payment Terms**: Hourly rate or retainer ($X/month for Y hours), invoicing net-15, expense reimbursement.
- **IP Rights**: Assignment of all work product; contractor retains license to portfolio use.
- **Confidentiality**: Broad definition of confidential info; survival 3 years.
- **Termination**: 30-day notice; for cause (breach, insolvency).
- **Non-Solicitation**: 12-month restriction on hiring each other's employees.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Mandatory mediation before litigation.

### Template 3: Creative/IP Intensive Agreement (Designers, Writers, Developers)
- **Scope of Work**: Deliverables acceptance criteria and revision limits (3 rounds).
- **Payment Terms**: Milestone payments tied to approval.
- **IP Rights**: Grant of license (non-exclusive, worldwide) to use deliverables; contractor retains copyright.
- **Moral Rights Waiver**: Client may modify work.
- **Confidentiality**: Limited to client business secrets.
- **Termination**: Automatic upon non-payment.

### Template 4: Service-Based Agreement (Consultants, Coaches, Virtual Assistants)
- **Scope of Work**: Service description, availability (e.g., 10 hours/week), communication methods.
- **Payment Terms**: Monthly retainer, auto-renewal clause.
- **IP Rights**: Client owns materials created; contractor owns methodology.
- **Confidentiality**: Client data protection clause.
- **Termination**: 30-day notice; refund for unused retainer.
- **Non-Compete**: Limited to direct competitors within 50 miles.

### Template 5: International Contractor Agreement
- **Scope of Work**: Multi-language deliverables.
- **Payment Terms**: Currency (USD/EUR), wire transfer fees, tax withholding responsibility.
- **IP Rights**: Compliance with local IP laws (e.g., EU copyright).
- **Confidentiality**: GDPR-compliant data processing addendum.
- **Termination**: Force majeure clause.
- **Governing Law**: UNCITRAL arbitration.

9. **Termination Letter Template**: For client or contractor.
10. **Dispute Resolution Agreement**: Mediation/arbitration clause.
11. **Data Protection Addendum**: GDPR-specific.
12. **Insurance Requirements Clause**: Liability, workers' comp.
13. **Indemnification Clause**: Mutual or one-way.
14. **Force Majeure Clause**: Pandemic, natural disasters.
15. **Entire Agreement Clause**: Supersedes prior agreements.
16. **Severability Clause**: If part invalid, rest stands.

### How to Use
- Fill in bracketed fields (e.g., [Client Name], [Date]).
- Choose template based on project type.
- Consult a lawyer for high-value or complex contracts.
- Keep signed copies for 7 years.
