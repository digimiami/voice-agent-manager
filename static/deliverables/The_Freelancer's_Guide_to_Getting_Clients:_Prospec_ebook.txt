**Chapter 1: Prospecting**
1. Define your ideal client profile (industry, size, budget, pain points).
2. Use LinkedIn advanced search to find decision-makers.
3. Create a list of 50 target clients per week using tools like Hunter.io or Apollo.
4. Warm outreach strategy: Engage with their content before pitching.
5. Cold email template: Subject line: [Specific problem] + [Your solution]. Body: 3 sentences max, value-first.

**Chapter 2: Networking**
6. Attend 2 virtual or local events per month (e.g., Meetup, Eventbrite).
7. The 3-2-1 networking rule: 3 questions, 2 listens, 1 value statement.
8. Follow-up template: Reference a shared interest, offer a free resource.
9. Build referral partnerships with complementary freelancers.

**Chapter 3: Portfolio Building**
10. Case study structure: Problem → Process → Results (use numbers).
11. Include 3 diverse projects showing different skills.
12. Add testimonials with specific outcomes (e.g., 'Increased sales by 30%').
13. Create a one-page PDF portfolio for quick sharing.

**Chapter 4: Pitching**
14. Research the client's business before every pitch.
15. Pitch framework: Hook (pain point) → Insight (your unique approach) → Outcome (ROI).
16. Use a pricing table: Basic, Standard, Premium options.
17. Send a video Loom pitch for higher engagement.

**Chapter 5: Closing**
18. Discovery call script: 'What’s your biggest challenge with [topic]?'
19. Objection handling: 'I understand your concern about budget. Let me show how this pays for itself in 3 months.'
20. Proposal template: Executive summary, scope, timeline, pricing, next steps.
21. Follow-up sequence: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 (add value each time).
22. Close with a clear call to action: 'Reply to this email with 'Yes' to start.'
